SCHEDULE

  1. Aug – Sept

    • We unload and test our diverse range of imports
    • We pack and send goods to customers

  2. Early – Mid-Oct

    • Installation schedule is finalised and cannot be changed without difficulty
    • Risk assessments conducted and order fulfillment prepared

  3. Mid-Oct – Nov

    • Priority installs in mid-October
    • Main block of installs at very high volume throughout November

  4. Dec – Jan

    • The last installs are done on Christmas Day and the first dismantles on Boxing Day.
    • The last dismantles are for Orthodox Christmas and completed by January 10.
    • The remainder of January is assigned to stock maintenance and care.

  5. Feb – Apr

    • We attend international trade fairs to update our planning and design by learning from our peers.
    • After we return, we provide customers access to our European imports, for which a 6 month lead time is required for best pricing, and a 3 month lead time is essential.

  6. May – Jul

    • Christmas in July, an Australian wintertime festival focusing on ice skating and lighting exhibits.
    • We also provide our main tenders to Councils during this period, to serve communities across NSW.
